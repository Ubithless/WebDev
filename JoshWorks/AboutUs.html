About Us

Joshworks is a graphic design studio based in Iloilo City, Philippines. With around seven years of design experience and a small team of specialists accross branding, illustration, layout, motion, and web, we help businesses schools, organizarions, and creatives build clear, memorable visual identities.

What We Do

Branding and visual identity, social media management, merchandise design, motion graphics, event and publication campaigns, yearbook and publication layout, and design training.

Our Goal

To deliver professional, original, human-made design that genuinely represents you. We treat every project as a partnership — we listen first, then craft. We use modern tools, including AI, only to support our process, never to replace the human creativity and craft at the heart of every project.
